## 4. Requirements & Engineering Challenges for Future E1 Real-Time Serving

Connecting the frozen E1 LightGBM model to the real-time streaming infrastructure presents a major engineering challenge: **E1 cannot score a raw 10-field Kafka event directly.** It strictly requires the exact 406-feature representation on which it was trained.

### Required Future Serving Pipeline:
```text
Kafka Event (e.g. from 'fraud-transactions')
      │
      ▼
Schema Normalization (typed numerical & categorical fields)
      │
      ▼
Historical / Entity Feature Hydration (enriching event with cardholder aggregates)
      │
      ▼
E1 preprocessing.joblib (fitted median imputations & label encodings)
      │
      ▼
Exactly 406 Canonical Features
      │
      ▼
E1 model.txt (lgb.Booster.predict)
      │
      ▼
Fraud Probability (p)
      │
      ▼
0.616521 Frozen Decision Threshold
      │
      ▼
Decision: APPROVED (p < 0.616521) / BLOCKED (p >= 0.616521)
```

### Safety Principles for Future Implementation:
1. **Isolated Service Directory:** Implement within an isolated `serving/` or `api/` directory with its own dependencies (FastAPI, uvicorn).
2. **Read-Only Artifact Mount:** Mount `experiments/E1_lightgbm/model.txt` and `preprocessing.joblib` as strictly read-only assets.
3. **Dedicated Engineering Phase:** Treat online feature hydration as a distinct engineering milestone; do not quietly patch or alter the frozen research experiment.
4. **Preserve Research Freeze:** Zero retraining of E1, zero modification of existing research files or evaluation protocols.
